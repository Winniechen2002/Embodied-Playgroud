# EP-100 definition

[Overview and sources](README.en.md) · [Machine-readable membership](tasks.json)

EP-100 selects 100 representative tasks from the full 410 environments, spanning five suites, 16 types and MuJoCo, Isaac Lab and ManiSkill. Selection balances shared-runtime accessibility, native difficulty ranges and embodiments, with most slots assigned to parallel grippers and representative dexterous samples. Use it for a full representative evaluation.

Definition: `paper-loaded-only-v1`.

## Size and use

| Tasks | Launch | Tower | Lock | Cube | Home | Groups | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | 34 | 21 | 16 | 12 | 17 | 100 | 400 |

Counts fix access, observation, tools, budgets and base seed. Launch uses `loaded` only (first shot preloaded; subsequent shots still require reloading), others `default`; every task has four conditions. For reference, Pass@2 / Pass@5 / Pass@10 use at most 800 / 2000 / 4000 valid attempts respectively. Declare the limit before running, stop each condition at first success and report each requested cutoff separately; actual usage may be lower. Infrastructure exclusions are recorded separately.

Average four conditions per task, tasks within each suite, then the five suites equally (20% each). Do not pool all conditions for the headline score. See the [overview](README.en.md) for workload formulas, within-type split/tie rules, exception accounting and provenance.
