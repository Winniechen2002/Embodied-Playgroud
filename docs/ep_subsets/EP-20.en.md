# EP-20 definition

[Overview and sources](README.en.md) · [Machine-readable membership](tasks.json)

EP-20 is a fixed compact diagnostic subset of ten Easy and ten Hard tasks. It covers all 16 EP-100 types, its robot embodiments and three simulators; six Launch scenes span native tiers 1–5. The paper states that half of its ten Hard tasks had at least one pilot success on the same or closely related scenes. This motivates selection and does not establish success on all formal conditions. Since pilot evidence partly informed selection, EP-20 is not an unbiased estimate of full EP-100 performance.

Definition: `paper-loaded-only-v1`.

## Size and use

| Tasks | Launch | Tower | Lock | Cube | Home | Groups | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 6 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 20 | 80 |

Counts fix access, observation, tools, budgets and base seed. Launch uses `loaded` only (first shot preloaded; subsequent shots still require reloading), others `default`; every task has four conditions. Pass@2 uses at most 160 valid attempts, stopping at first success; actual usage may be lower. Infrastructure exclusions are recorded separately.

Average four conditions per task, tasks within each suite, then the five suites equally (20% each). Do not pool all conditions for the headline score. See the [overview](README.en.md) for workload formulas, within-type split/tie rules, exception accounting and provenance.
